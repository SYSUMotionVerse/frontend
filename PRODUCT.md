# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students following assigned exercise sessions in a WeChat Mini Program. They need to start training quickly, understand what comes next, and review progress without interpreting a dense analytics dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Help students complete guided exercise safely and consistently, then make adherence, training history, assessments, and progress easy to review.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, and direct. The product should feel supportive and focused rather than competitive or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style card grids, repeated summaries of the same data, saturated blue controls, decorative dashboards, oversized headings, and interfaces that make routine actions feel complex.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current student task before decoration.
2. Show one useful summary, then offer a clear path to detail.
3. Keep training states and progress language concrete.
4. Use familiar mini-program interactions and consistent controls.
5. Preserve calm spacing and strong legibility during movement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable type, strong contrast, sufficiently large touch targets, text labels for important states, and motion only when it communicates a state change. Do not rely on color alone.
